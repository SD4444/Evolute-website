## Design Context

### Users

The primary audience is ambitious deep-tech founders and leadership teams considering a capital raise, transaction, or strategic finance engagement. Investors, funds, and family offices are a secondary audience. Visitors need to understand Evolute's fit, expertise, services, and credibility quickly, then find a clear path to a relevant detail page or conversation.

### Brand Personality

Restrained, technical, and human. Evolute should feel data-driven and deeply competent without becoming institutional, cold, or sales-led. The emotional goal is quiet confidence and clarity.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light, editorial interface with generous but controlled space, strong sentence-case typography, thin rules, and minimal decoration. Innovation Industries' About page is a reference for progressive disclosure: concise indexed rows remain visually quiet until a visitor chooses to reveal detail. Evolute's Academic Blue, Frosted Almond, Space Grotesk, Inter, and industry-specific accents remain authoritative.

### Design Principles

1. Reveal depth on demand. Keep summaries short and place supporting detail inside accessible disclosure rows.
2. Prefer structure over containers. Use typography, alignment, and rules before cards, shadows, or decorative backgrounds.
3. Give each section one clear purpose and one primary next step.
4. Preserve industry color as contextual information, never generic decoration.
5. Meet WCAG AA, support keyboard navigation and reduced motion, and keep critical information available without hover.
