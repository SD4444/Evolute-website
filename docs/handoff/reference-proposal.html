02 · The asset

This is not a revenue story.
It is proven IP the industry is adopting.

The moat
Patented, and hard to copy.

Granted patents on process and product globally since 2013, deepened by offline production tradecraft no filing reveals.

The proof
Accredited where it counts.

Fire, blast and EMI shielding in one thin material. Withstands ~2,000°C for 2+ hours, meets MoD DEF STAN, exceeds Porsche and Hyundai EMI requirements. BAE Systems Type 26 approval in train.

The tailwind
Circular and sovereign.

The only fully recyclable product in its class, built on a NATO-domiciled supply chain. New fire, sustainability and localisation rules lock lower-cost Asian competitors out.

Saving weight. Saving emissions. Saving lives. The buyer acquires a decade of certified engineering, not a P&L.
03 · Why now

Four forces converge on this technology.

Regulation

Fire compliance is now a purchase. Two-hour battery fire regulations and defence DEF STAN standards make certified protection mandatory across EV, energy storage and naval programmes.

Rearmament

NATO localisation is policy. Sovereign supply chains are a procurement requirement, and CFP’s value chain is fully NATO-domiciled while Chinese alternatives are excluded.

Electrification

~£10bn of combined end markets by 2030. EV platforms, energy storage, data centres, high-speed rail and electric aerospace all need thin multifunctional protection.

Consolidation

Strategics buy proven niche technology. Materials groups and primes acquire certified, program-embedded platforms rather than spending years building and accrediting their own.

Sources: CFP Composites investor materials, May 2026.
04 · The transaction

One route: a strategic majority sale.

Not a funding round, and not a financial buyout. A strategic acquirer converts CFP’s credibility gap into contract wins from day one, and pays for what the technology is worth to them.

Perimeter
A majority transfer of ~75% of the shares, structured with earn-outs or milestones where that bridges value expectations.
Management
Stays and rolls equity. Continuity of the team is a condition of the process, not a concession.
The buyer brings
Balance sheet and name for tenders, channel access into primes and OEMs, and scale-up capital for localised capacity.
The process
Confidential and curated. A small, senior-led field of 8–12 qualified strategics, run in parallel to keep competitive tension.
Timing
6–9 months from kickoff to completion. Target closing Q1–Q2 2027.

02 · Tier 1 manufacturers

03 · Defence & maritime

Primes and their suppliers around live naval and defence programmes, where DEF STAN accreditation and sovereign supply are the entry ticket.

04 · Strategic capital

Industrial holdings and dual-use investors backing NATO-aligned manufacturing, as sponsor alongside a strategic operator.
